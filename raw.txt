💰 Position Sizing — The Rule That Saves You
Fix your risk at ₹X per trade — same every single trade

Step 1: Decide max daily loss limit
        Example: ₹10,000/day max loss

Step 2: Risk per trade = Daily limit ÷ 5
        = ₹10,000 ÷ 5 = ₹2,000 per trade

Step 3: Shares = Risk per trade ÷ (Entry - SL)

Example Sammaan:
= 2,000 ÷ (161.39 - 158.76)
= 2,000 ÷ 2.63
= 760 shares maximum
This way Sammaan can go to ZERO and you only lose ₹2,000 — not your entire day's profit.

📤 Exit Plan — 3 Tier System
Tier 1 — At 1:1 Risk Reward

Book 40% of position
Move SL to breakeven on remaining
Tier 2 — At 2:1 Risk Reward

Book another 40% of position
Trail SL below last higher low (longs) or higher high (shorts)
Tier 3 — Remaining 20%

Let it run with trailing SL
This is your "home run" portion
Exit only when trailing SL is hit
